The Challenge

A premium salon needs more than just a standard text list of services. Hair III Studio required an immersive visual experience that builds anticipation and trust before the client even walks through the door, paired with a completely frictionless way to book local appointments.

The Direction

We utilized a stark, dark-mode aesthetic paired with bold, brutalist typography to make their high-quality styling photography pop. Instead of traditional lists, we built highly visual, interactive masonry grids for the "Portfolio" and "Services" sections. We then streamlined the user journey by integrating clear "Working Hours" tables and an interactive location map directly above the booking call-to-action.

The Result

A bold, edgy brand identity that instantly sets the studio apart from local competitors. By removing digital friction and answering logistical questions instantly, the new layout significantly boosted the conversion rate for online appointment bookings.

The Anatomy of a Modern Salon Site

A premium salon needs more than just a text list of services. It requires an immersive visual experience that builds anticipation and trust before the client even walks through the door, paired with an effortless booking journey.

01

High-Contrast Editorial Design

We utilized a stark, dark-mode aesthetic paired with bold, brutalist typography. This dramatic contrast makes the high-quality photography of their styling work pop, immediately establishing a high-end, edgy brand identity that sets them apart from local competitors.

02

Immersive Service Grids

Instead of standard text lists, we built highly visual, interactive masonry grids for the "Portfolio" and "Services" sections. This layout allows prospective clients to visually explore hair transformations, increasing engagement and time-on-site.

03

Frictionless Local Booking

We integrated clear "Working Hours" tables and an interactive location map directly above the final booking CTA. By removing friction and answering logistical questions instantly, we significantly boosted the conversion rate for local appointment bookings.

The Result
“A website that feels as luxurious and bold as the services they provide.”
