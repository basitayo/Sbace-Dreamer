The Challenge

The client needed to separate themselves from standard local contractors. Their previous digital presence was outdated and failed to communicate the high-end nature of their architectural and residential work.

The Direction

We adopted a highly spacious, editorial grid. By stripping away heavy UI elements and focusing entirely on large-scale photography and crisp typography, the brand instantly felt more authoritative.

The Anatomy of a High-Converting Site

Homeowners are deeply protective of their property. To win high-ticket exterior and cabinet painting jobs, a contractor's website must eliminate anxiety and manufacture trust within the first 3 seconds of a visit.

01

Manufacturing Instant Trust

We didn't just place a hero image; we engineered a "Trust Strip" above the fold. By immediately presenting floating badges—"4.9 Rating," "320+ Homes Painted," and "Licensed & Insured"—we answer the homeowner's most critical subconscious questions instantly. This layout immediately validates their premium pricing and prevents price-shopping.

02

Interactive Proof of Craftsmanship

Painting is a visual industry, yet most contractors rely on static, low-quality galleries. We integrated a dynamic Before/After interactive slider for the "Signature Transformations" section. This interactive element keeps users on the page longer, increasing engagement metrics, and provides undeniable, psychological proof of the contractor's capability.

03

Frictionless User Journeys

A confused mind says no. We implemented a sticky navigation bar with a highly visible, contrasting "Get Free Estimate" CTA. Paired with a massive, four-step numbered "Process" section, we demystified the customer journey. When homeowners know exactly what happens after they click "Contact," conversion rates skyrocket.
