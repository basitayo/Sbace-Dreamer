Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to move my furniture before you begin?

No. Our white-glove service includes carefully moving, wrapping, and protecting all heavy furniture and delicate items before prep begins.

What level of surface preparation do you perform?

We are uncompromising on prep. This includes scraping, skim-coating, caulking, and HEPA vacuum sanding to ensure a perfectly smooth substrate before any paint is applied.
