Engineering a B2B Lead Generation Engine

Selling to businesses requires a fundamentally different architecture than selling to homeowners. The site needs to serve as a comprehensive capabilities deck, proving compliance, efficiency, and industry-specific expertise at a glance.

01

Industry-Specific Authority

Commercial clients need to know you understand their specific environment. We designed a clear "Industries We Serve" taxonomy, allowing users in healthcare, retail, or industrial sectors to instantly self-identify and verify the contractor's relevant experience.

02

The B2B Trust Matrix

Instead of focusing just on aesthetics, we prioritized operational metrics. By heavily featuring sections like "Execution Process," massive numerical milestones (200+ Projects, 2M+ Sq Ft), and notable "Client Partners," we systematically dismantled the buyer's perceived risk.

03

Deep-Dive Project Showcases

A simple photo isn't enough for a $100k+ contract. We structured the "Project Showcase" like mini case studies within the site itself—highlighting the square footage, materials used, and specific challenges overcome, providing the exact data points GCs need to justify their hiring decisions.
